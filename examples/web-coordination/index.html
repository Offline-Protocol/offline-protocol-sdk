Device coordination

Open this page in two or more tabs. Each tab is a device: it publishes its own state, and subscribes to every other device it finds. Updates arrive as they happen, sealed end to end in the 1:1 session between the two devices. A device that missed some gets the state back at its next renewal, or when you press Resync.
